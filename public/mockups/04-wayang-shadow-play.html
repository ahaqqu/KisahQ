Garis & Motif · Line & Motif

Perforasi titik-titik kecil (corak batik) menembus siluet; rim emas tipis di tepi wayang; bingkai kayu ukir; bau dagu wayang (tongkat) emas. Wajah tanpa detail — ekspresi lewat pose + titik emas. Fine batik-like perforation punched through the silhouette; thin gold rims; carved wooden frame; gold puppet rod. Faces stay abstract — expression comes from pose + gold dots.
